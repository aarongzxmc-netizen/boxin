About BOXIN

Professional Manufacturer of Stainless Steel Waste Bins

Foshan Boxin Environmental Protection Equipment Co., Ltd. was established in 2010 and specializes in stainless steel commercial trash bins, outdoor waste bins, indoor garbage bins, recycling bins and related waste management products.

Located in Foshan, Guangdong, our factory enjoys convenient access to Guangzhou Port and Shenzhen Port. We support OEM / ODM, bulk orders and project supply for customers in Middle East & Europe.
