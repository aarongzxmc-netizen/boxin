FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about BOXIN stainless steel commercial bins, customization, orders and after-sales support.

What is your MOQ?

Our standard MOQ is 20 PCS. For customized models, MOQ may vary based on size, finish, logo and structure.

Do you support OEM / ODM?

Yes. BOXIN supports OEM / ODM, including logo, labels, size, opening type, color marks and packaging.

Can you provide Arabic / English labels?

Yes. We can provide Arabic / English bilingual labels and other languages according to project needs.

What materials do you use?

We mainly offer stainless steel commercial bins, including 201 and 304 stainless steel options depending on the model and application.

Which markets do you mainly export to?

Our major markets are the Middle East and Europe, including Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and other regions.

What products do you manufacture?

We make recycling bins, lobby bins, pedal bins, ashtray bins, outdoor bins and custom stainless steel bins.

How long is the lead time?

Lead time depends on order quantity and customization. Standard orders are usually faster, while OEM projects require sample confirmation and production scheduling.

Can you provide samples?

Yes, sample support is available for selected models. Please contact us with the model you need.

Still have questions?

Our team is here to help you choose the right solutions for your project. Send us your product requirements and quantity for a fast quotation.
