# Product Description
The BX-OD02 is a modern, matte-black waste bin engineered for outdoor public environments. Robust construction coupled with clean minimal lines fits beautifully into municipal and commercial parklands.

## Key Features
- High-durability galvanized steel core with thick weatherproof matte black powder coating.
- Top shield lid blocks direct rainwater and snow entry.
- Side lock door prevents unauthorized opening or vandalism.
- Floor mounting flange for secure, windproof, and theft-proof anchoring.
