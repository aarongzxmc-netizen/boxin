# Product Description
The BX-RC01 is a premium 4-stream recycling station designed for high-traffic public areas. Featuring an angled top design, it prevents waste accumulation on top of the bin.

## Key Features
- High-grade 304 stainless steel construction for maximum durability and rust resistance.
- 4 clear waste streams with customizable bilingual Arabic / English sorting labels.
- Soft-close mechanisms and ergonomic top openings.
- Lockable front door for easy maintenance and secure operation.
